# Privacy

This project has no analytics, background telemetry or maintainer-operated cloud service. Mock planning is local.

Real composition may read local videos, screenshots, audio, captions and branding assets. Those files can contain personal, confidential or proprietary information. Keep them under `.runtime/` or another ignored workspace and delete them when no longer needed.

The maintainers do not receive local assets or renders. Any renderer, storage provider, font service, stock library or media API enabled by the operator may process data under its own privacy notice and account settings.

Review every frame, caption, audio track and metadata field before publishing. Redaction, cropping and safe-zone rules reduce risk but do not guarantee privacy.
