# Responsible Use Policy

Use this project only with media, identities, music, fonts, brands and source recordings you own or are authorized to use.

## Allowed

- Tutorials, educational videos, documentation, internal training and lawful marketing.
- Composing authorized avatar, browser, VideoVox, image, diagram, B-roll and caption assets.
- Generating disclosed synthetic or edited media.

## Not allowed

- Deceptive edits, fabricated evidence, impersonation or misleading endorsements.
- Removing attribution or rights information when required by a source license.
- Publishing private, copyrighted or regulated material without authorization.
- Using unlicensed music, fonts, stock media, trademarks, voices or likenesses.
- Misrepresenting a mock, staged or generated result as a verified real-world event.

## Required safeguards

- Preserve asset provenance in manifests.
- Review rights, privacy, captions and factual claims before rendering and publication.
- Keep publication as a separate human approval step.
